Admissions through IITVEEE

About the Exam

The Interregional Institute of Technologia, Vijayavada Entrance Exam (IITVEEE) is a competitive examination for admission to our undergraduate programs. The exam tests your proficiency in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.

Exam Pattern

  • Duration: 3 hours
  • Mode: Online
  • Question Type: Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ)

Syllabus

The syllabus for the IITVEEE is based on the 11th and 12th-grade curriculum of major national boards.
