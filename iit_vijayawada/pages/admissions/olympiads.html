Admissions through International Olympiads

Eligibility

Students who have qualified in the following International Olympiads are eligible to apply through this pathway:

  • International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO)
  • International Physics Olympiad (IPhO)
  • International Astronomy Olympiad (IAO)

Please check the specific requirements for each Olympiad.

Application Process

1. Fill out the online application form.

2. Upload your Olympiad qualification certificate.

3. Pay the application fee.

4. Shortlisted candidates will be called for an interview.
