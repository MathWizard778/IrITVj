Admissions through Professor Recommendation

Eligibility

This pathway is for students who have received a strong recommendation from a professor at the Interregional Institute of Technologia or the Interregional Institute of Secular Colours.

Application Process

1. The recommending professor must nominate the student.

2. The student must then fill out the online application form.

3. The application will be reviewed by the admissions committee.

4. Shortlisted candidates may be called for an interview.
